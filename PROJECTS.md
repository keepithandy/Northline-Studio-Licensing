## Status vocabulary

- **Active flagship:** primary maintained project.
- **Active supporting project:** maintained reusable engine, tool, or companion project.
- **Experimental alpha:** playable or testable but explicitly early-stage.
- **Private:** not described publicly beyond an approved neutral status.
- **Archived:** retained for history and no longer actively maintained.
